How to play

Classic Ludo rules, with the dice rolled securely on the game server.
01Roll a 6 to bring a token out of base.
02Tap a highlighted token to move it clockwise.
03Capture an opponent on an unprotected square to send it home.
04Six gives another roll. Three sixes in a row forfeit the turn.
05Reach the centre with an exact roll. First to bring all four tokens home wins.

Star squares are safe. Both players must be online to take their turns.
