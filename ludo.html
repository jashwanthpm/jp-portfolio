Classic Ludo rules

Choose 2, 3 or 4 players. Every roll and move is checked by the game server.
01Roll a 6 to bring a token out of base.
02Move clockwise. Tap a highlighted token to move it.
03Star squares are safe. Land on an unprotected opponent to send their token home.
04Two or more tokens form a blockade; tokens cannot pass it.
05Six, a capture, or reaching home earns another roll. Three consecutive sixes forfeit the turn.
06Reach the centre with an exact roll. First to bring all four tokens home wins.

The “My side” switch only rotates your board view. It never changes the turn order or token locations.
